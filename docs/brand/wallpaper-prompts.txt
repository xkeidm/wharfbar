GENERAL RULES
- Output: 3840x2160 (16:9). For ultrawide, ask for 21:9.
- NEVER let the AI draw the icon. Generate the wallpaper without a logo, then place the logo on top yourself.
- Logo files (docs/brand/logo/):
    Dark background : wharfbar-mark-small-on-dark.svg
    Light background : wharfbar-mark-small-on-light.svg
- Logo size: 10-15% of the screen width (6-8% in the plain/muted version, at 60-70% opacity).
- Keep the middle and side areas plain: the top bar sits at the top and the dock at the bottom.
- Every prompt has a "REFERENCE" line that names the file to upload to the AI.

PALETTE
- Dark : bg #182E3F | pier #EAF0F5 | sea lines #306791 | sun #F2B66B
- Light: bg #EAF1F6 | pier #17374F | sea lines #9BBBD4 | sun #E59A45
- Accent: #0078D4

NEGATIVE PROMPT (in tools that support it)
text, logo, watermark, people, boats, buildings, cluttered, oversaturated, lens flare, low contrast
